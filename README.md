# SiriDev Mail

**SiriDev Mail** is a private, catch-all disposable email inbox powered by **`siridev.me`**. It lets you create and use unlimited email addresses on the fly—without manually signing up for new accounts or going through phone number verification.

---

## How It Works

1. **Every address works automatically:** Any email sent to `<anything>@siridev.me` (for example, `shopping@siridev.me` or `swift-fox42@siridev.me`) is caught automatically by **Cloudflare Email Routing**.
2. **Delivered straight to your inbox:** Incoming messages are parsed and forwarded securely to your private **SiriDev Mail** web inbox.
3. **Read and copy in seconds:** Your inbox refreshes automatically every 5 seconds, highlights verification codes (OTPs) for one-click copying, and displays HTML emails safely.

Because `siridev.me` is a private domain rather than a public disposable-email service, websites treat your addresses like normal email addresses.

---

## Key Features

- **One-Click Random Addresses:** Generate a fresh address anytime with the **New Email** button and copy it in one click.
- **Custom Aliases:** Type any name you want (such as `newsletter`, `trial`, or `store`) to instantly create `<name>@siridev.me`.
- **Catch-All View:** Switch to **All Mail (`*@siridev.me`)** to see every incoming email—even if you made up an address on a signup form without creating it in the app first.
- **Automatic Verification Code Detection:** 4-to-8 digit OTP and login codes are automatically detected and shown in a quick-copy badge.
- **Saved Browser Inboxes:** Your generated and custom addresses are remembered in your browser so you can switch between them or remove them anytime.
- **Search & Cleanup:** Search across senders, subjects, and verification codes, or delete individual emails and clear an inbox with one click.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Open your inbox** and enter your access passcode if prompted.
2. **Pick or create an address:**
   - Click **New Email** to generate a random address, or
   - Enter a word under **Create Custom Alias** to make a specific one.
3. **Click Copy** and paste the address into any website or newsletter signup.
4. **Read your email:** Within seconds, incoming messages appear automatically. Click any detected OTP badge to copy the verification code right away.

---

## Privacy & Good to Know

- **Passcode Protected:** When protected with a passcode, only people with the passcode can open the inbox or read incoming messages.
- **Receive-Only:** SiriDev Mail is built for receiving signups, verification links, OTP codes, and notifications—it does not send outgoing emails.
- **Safe Email Viewing:** HTML emails are rendered inside a restricted sandbox so embedded scripts cannot run.
- **Message Retention:** The inbox keeps the most recent 500 emails and automatically clears older ones as new mail arrives.
